Commercial rent reviews • Tenants • Leeds

Facing a rent review? We help tenants protect their position.

T1 Asset Management supports commercial tenants across Leeds and Yorkshire through rent reviews and lease renewals. We focus on lease wording, time limits, and robust comparable evidence to help challenge excessive demands and negotiate a fair open-market rent.

Contact us

If you’re a commercial tenant in Leeds (or UK-wide) facing a rent review, the lease terms and strict time limits can materially affect the outcome. Early advice helps you avoid missed deadlines and maintain negotiation leverage.

As specialist commercial rent review surveyors, we analyse the lease wording and evidence to challenge excessive proposals and work towards a fair open-market rent.

Many rent reviews are ‘upward only’, so our aim is to minimise any increase — and where the lease allows, ensure your rent reflects current market realities. On renewals, it may be possible to negotiate improvements to the overall terms, including rent, depending on market conditions.

Whether you occupy retail, restaurant, office, healthcare or industrial/warehouse space, our role is to make the process clear and manageable — and to negotiate firmly on your behalf.

Where appropriate, we identify practical solutions that can reduce cost exposure and protect business continuity.

If you’re facing a significant rent increase, or navigating the complexities of the Landlord & Tenant Act 1954 during a renewal, we can guide you through the strategy and evidence required.

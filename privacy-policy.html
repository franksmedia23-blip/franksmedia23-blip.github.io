Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 15th, 2025

T1 Asset Management ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 when you use our website.

1. Who We Are

T1 Asset Management is a commercial property consultancy based at Unit 19 Holly Park Mills, Calverley, Leeds, LS28 5QS. For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at rob@t1assetmanagement.co.uk. We are the data controller responsible for the personal data collected through this website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you use our website:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Messages submitted via our contact form

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about it
  • Provide the information or services you have requested

We do not use your enquiry data for marketing, and we will not sell, share, or pass your data to any third party for marketing purposes. The lawful basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services, and, where you have made contact, taking steps at your request prior to entering into a possible engagement.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We use a small number of third-party service providers to operate this website. Your data may be processed by:

  • Formspree (formspree.io), which processes and stores submissions from our contact and callback forms on our behalf. Their privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google, via reCAPTCHA, for spam and abuse prevention on our forms (see section 7).
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, where we are required to disclose information by law.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. All processors are required to handle your data in line with UK data protection law.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry data for up to 2 years, after which it is deleted, unless an ongoing business relationship or a legal obligation requires us to keep it for longer.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at rob@t1assetmanagement.co.uk. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are unhappy with how we have handled your data.

7. Spam Protection (Google reCAPTCHA)

Our contact and callback forms are protected by Google reCAPTCHA, which helps us prevent automated abuse and spam. When you interact with these forms, reCAPTCHA may collect information about your device and activity, including your IP address, browser and operating system details, and interactions with the page, and process this information on Google's servers to distinguish genuine enquiries from automated submissions.

This use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms of Service. We use the data collected solely for spam prevention and do not use it for any other purpose.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data from unauthorised access or misuse. This website is served over a secure HTTPS connection, and form submissions are transmitted securely to Formspree.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, contact: rob@t1assetmanagement.co.uk
